# FishHaven — Privacy Notice for Parents & Guardians (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Framework-drafted for India + US (UK/EU excluded). Needs counsel
> sign-off and a final published version (plain language; **[LEGAL]** Indian-language versions).

**Who we are.** FishHaven is a Christian community platform. You hold the account; your children
use it as **profiles** under your account. Children do not have their own logins.

**Your consent is required.** A child's profile stays inactive until you, the parent/guardian,
give consent. You confirm you are the child's parent or legal guardian. You can withdraw consent
at any time — doing so deactivates the profile and deletes the child's data.

**What we collect about a child — and nothing more:**
- A display name and avatar you choose.
- An age range (under 13, or 13–17) so we apply the right safety rules.
- Activity needed to run the service and keep them safe (e.g. pages viewed, kindness "strikes").
We do **not** collect your child's real name, date of birth, location, or contact details.

**What we never do.** We do not track or profile children, show them targeted ads, sell or share
their data, or use it to train AI models.

**How we keep children safe.** Content and any contact involving children is monitored; child-to-child
connections and messages require your approval and are supervised. We are legally required to report
suspected child-abuse material to authorities (NCMEC).

**Your rights as a parent.** From the family/admin area you can **see, export, correct, and delete**
everything we hold about your child, and withdraw consent. We keep child data only as long as needed,
then erase it (except records the law requires us to keep, such as a safety report).

**Where the service is offered.** FishHaven is available in India, the United States, and most other
regions, but **not in the UK or EU/EEA** at this time.

**Security & breaches.** We protect data with least-privilege access and encryption in transit, and
will notify you and the relevant authority of a breach affecting your child's data.

**Questions or complaints.** Contact our grievance/support channel at [CONTACT]. **[LEGAL: India may
require a named Grievance Officer + address.]**

**Changes.** We'll notify you and re-request consent if we materially change what we collect or why.
